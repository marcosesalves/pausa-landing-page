Why Pausa exists

Working at a screen, hours slip by in one long stretch. Stopping for a few breaths now and then is easy to agree with and easy to forget.

Pausa is a small, kind reminder to take that pause, and a friendly face to take it with.

Pausa is a small experiment in taking small pauses seriously. I hope it makes your day feel a little lighter.

Marcos Alves, maker of Pausa

Privacy note

On your Mac. Your settings and Pip's garden are stored on your computer, in the app's own folder. Pausa has no accounts and no analytics, and this page loads nothing from other websites, not even fonts.

Feedback is optional. If you choose to send feedback or a bug report, it contains what you write, plus only the extras you leave ticked: the app and macOS version, a few settings (never Pip's name), recent app events, and a screenshot if you attach one. You see everything before it is sent. A random install id, which is not linked to you, lets reports from one install be grouped.

Where it goes. Reports are stored on a Supabase project hosted in the EU (Ireland) and used only to improve Pausa during the beta. Please don't include customer or personal data. To have a report deleted, email pausa.companion.app@gmail.com.
